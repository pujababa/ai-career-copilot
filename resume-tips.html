Resume & Interview Preparation

Practical advice for students to build strong resumes and prepare for interviews.

AJ
Alex Johnson Student · Data Science

Resume Tips

Keep resume limited to 1 page

Concise resumes get read. Focus on your strongest, most relevant experience for each role.

Highlight technical projects

Showcase real projects with clear outcomes, tech stack, and your specific contributions.

Add skills and certifications

List relevant technologies, tools, and certifications that match the job description.

Use clear formatting

Use consistent headings, bullet points, and whitespace for easy scanning by recruiters.

Include GitHub or portfolio links

Let employers see your code and projects. A live portfolio or GitHub profile strengthens your application.

Interview Tips

Practice data structures and algorithms

Solve problems regularly on LeetCode or similar. Focus on arrays, trees, graphs, and common patterns.

Prepare common HR questions

Practice answering "Tell me about yourself", "Why this company?", and behavioral STAR examples.

Explain your projects clearly

Be ready to walk through problem, solution, tech choices, challenges, and what you learned.

Learn system design basics

Understand scalability, databases, APIs, and how to sketch high-level system architectures.

Maintain confidence during interviews

Speak clearly, think aloud when solving problems, and stay calm. It's okay to ask for clarification.
